Content Projection

Use ng-content to let a reusable component provide structure while its parent provides the specific content.

How the card receives content

  1. The card declares ng-content placeholders.
  2. Selectors such as [card-title] place matching elements in named slots.
  3. Unmatched elements are projected into the card body.
