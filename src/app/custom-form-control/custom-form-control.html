Angular forms

Custom Form Controls

A custom control can work with Angular forms when it implements the ControlValueAccessor contract.

How the rating joins the form

  1. writeValue() receives the value held by the form control.
  2. The rating calls Angular’s change callback when a star is selected.
  3. The form’s validator decides whether the selected rating is valid.
